Tips & Tricks: Maximizing Your Secret Weapon

HOW TO MAXIMIZE YOUR SECRET WEAPON: DAILY ROUTINE

Follow this recommended routine to get the most out of your Secret Weapon and keep your key levels organized throughout the major trading sessions.

6:00 AM – 7:00 AM | Initial Rest Period Allow the system to rest before starting your daily analysis routine.
7:30 AM – Before 8:00 AM | Asia/Tokyo Session Preparation Sync your Secret Weapon to generate the key levels for the 8:00 AM Asia/Tokyo session. These levels will serve as your initial reference points for the session.
8:00 AM – 2:00 PM | Monitor and Compare Key Levels You may sync the system whenever you need updated analysis. Compare the newly generated key levels with your original Asia/Tokyo session levels.

Observe which levels are respected or broken by price action. This helps you monitor how the market reacts to your initial reference levels throughout the session.
3:00 PM | London Session Preparation Sync your Secret Weapon again to generate updated key levels ahead of the 3:30 PM London session. Use these levels as your new reference points.
4:00 PM – 8:00 PM | Monitor and Compare Key Levels Continue syncing whenever necessary. Compare the updated levels with your previous analysis and observe how price reacts to the identified zones.
9:00 PM | London–New York Overlap Preparation Sync your Secret Weapon to generate fresh key levels before the 9:30 PM London–New York session overlap. These levels will serve as your reference for the next phase of market activity.
10:00 PM Onward | Final Monitoring and Comparison Continue syncing as needed and compare the latest key levels with your previous results. Monitor price reactions and use the information to support your trading decisions.

IMPORTANT REMINDER

Consistency is key. Always compare newly generated key levels with your previous analysis instead of relying solely on the latest results. Use price action and proper risk management to validate your trading decisions.
